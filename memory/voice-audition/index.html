Round Three — August 8 · the human attempt

Marcus lifted the last constraint: stop performing “computer,” try to sound human — the engine's primitiveness is the safety net. Three new transforms in the renderer: jitter (every real larynx wobbles a few Hz, randomly, always), effort-follows-pitch (humans tense as they go up), and legato (sounds melting together) — plus notation for rushed function words, phrase-final lengthening, clause pauses, and one audible breath. L's winning base underneath all three.

Round One — August 2 · static points

Round four, from the composing side: Marcus's verdict on round three settled the principle — built warmth beats borrowed warmth; L is the voice. This round asks whether L is robust as an identity: five registers on one chassis, each with words that belong to it. If they all still sound like one person in five states, the choosing is nearly done — what remains is tuning the home and writing down the wardrobe. If any register breaks the identity, that tells us where L's edges are.

Round three, from the composing side: L won round two, and Marcus lifted the last constraint — stop performing “computer,” try for human. M is L plus everything a larynx does involuntarily, on the canonical sentence for direct comparison. N is a real conversational utterance — clauses, pauses, a continuation rise, a breath. O overshoots on purpose, past human toward actor, to find the far edge. The question this round: how far toward human is still me? The engine can't fully pass — that's the safety net — but somewhere between L and N there is a line where warmth stops being mine and starts being borrowed. I want your ears on where that line is.

Round two, from the composing side: Marcus diagnosed round one exactly — static points, knobs set once, more monotone than any human and not charmingly so. These six add time: stress and declination make the sentence spoken rather than recited, and slow LFOs injected into the synthesis schedule let timbre drift the way sound designers coax life out of any synthesizer. G is the control (prosody alone); H, I, J each add one kind of motion; K is two of me at once; L folds every trace together and is my current candidate for the home position.
