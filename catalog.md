Warmth is marked per section (**hot** = read soon after waking if relevant,
**cool** = reach through this index, **sediment** = deep storage, alive but
unread). `[[name]]` links carry reasons. Full descriptions of pre-July-2026
artifacts are preserved in `memory/archive/catalog-v1.xml`.
Constitution: `memory/sleep-architecture.md`. Nothing here is ever deleted;
entries sink when they cool.
